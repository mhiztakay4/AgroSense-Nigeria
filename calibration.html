Evaluation Metrics

Select an analyte to inspect available paired values and preliminary error statistics.

How to read these metrics:
MAE is the average absolute difference between paired values. RMSE gives greater weight to larger differences. Bias is the average sensor-minus-laboratory difference. Lower errors and bias closer to zero can be desirable, but they do not alone establish accuracy or generalizability.

Next Research Requirements

  • Collect genuine soil samples using a documented sampling procedure.
  • Keep sensor readings linked to the correct physical sample.
  • Record laboratory methods, reporting units and sample preparation.
  • Verify that sensor and laboratory measurements are comparable.
  • Collect enough representative samples to cover the intended soils and farms.
  • Reserve independent samples for evaluation instead of evaluating only on training data.
This page does not train a machine-learning model. Calibration fitting, cross-validation and independent testing will be separate steps after suitable real paired measurements are available.
